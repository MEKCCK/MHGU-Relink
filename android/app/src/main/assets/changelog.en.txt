MHGU Relink 0.2.8 · 2026-11-22

2026-11-22: HUD size
Settings -> Display has HUD size (60-100%): the whole hunting HUD shrinks toward its corners - the bars, the item bar, the Hunter Arts, the map and the target camera - at once. 100% is exactly as before.

2026-11-22: Brick low-end tier
One step below Toaster: 15% 3D, 30 FPS, a 360-line HUD, no reverb or grass; the 3D resolution can now go down to 10%.

2026-11-22: Effects switch
Settings -> Graphics has Effects: off, the hits' sparks, the fire and the breath, the dust, the rain and the snow, and the monsters' and Hunter Arts' flashes are not drawn. Sounds, timing and the game's rules are unchanged, but a monster's attack warning is gone too.

2026-11-22: Textures on low-memory phones
Under 3.5 GB, one more level of the CPU-decoded 3D textures is dropped (about 1/16 of the memory); under 5 GB, Full-size textures keeps every level (needs a restart, takes much more memory, and the app may be closed).

2026-11-22: Upscale filter, present pacing, and PC Low-end resolution
Below 100% the 3D picture can be scaled Sharp (nearest); a frame cap that is a whole fraction of the screen's refresh rate now uses FIFO, for a steadier picture; on PC, Low-end mode asks for lines (720/540/360) instead of a percentage.
